### Gardens
- [Descanso Gardens](https://www.descansogardens.org/) — 150-acre botanical garden. [1418 Descanso Dr, La Cañada Flintridge, CA 91011](https://maps.google.com/?q=1418+Descanso+Dr,+La+Canada+Flintridge,+CA+91011) - [(818) 949-4200](tel:818-949-4200). Open daily 9 AM – 5 PM (closed Christmas Day). Advance tickets available online.
