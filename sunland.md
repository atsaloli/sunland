## Resources

### Food Banks
- Dial [211](tel:211) for current local food referrals, 24/7. Hours below come from directory listings and can change — call ahead.
- [Sunland-Tujunga Temporary Aid Center](https://locator.lacounty.gov/lac/Location/3173974/sunland---tujunga-temporary-aid-center) — emergency food for residents of Lakeview Terrace, Shadow Hills and Sunland-Tujunga, including people who are homeless or undocumented. [7747 Foothill Blvd, Tujunga, CA 91042](https://maps.google.com/?q=7747+Foothill+Blvd,+Tujunga,+CA+91042) - [(818) 352-2421](tel:818-352-2421)
- [Tujunga United Methodist Church (Bailey Human Care Center)](https://1degree.org/opp/get-free-food-tujunga-united-methodist-church-tujunga-ca-91042) — drive-up food bank, no eligibility or citizenship requirements. Wed and Fri 11 AM – 1 PM. [9901 Tujunga Canyon Blvd, Tujunga, CA 91042](https://maps.google.com/?q=9901+Tujunga+Canyon+Blvd,+Tujunga,+CA+91042) - [(818) 352-1481](tel:818-352-1481)
- Unity of Friends in Christ — LA Regional Food Bank partner. 2nd and 4th Friday, 10 AM – 12 PM; walk-ins accepted. [9100 Tujunga Canyon Blvd, Tujunga, CA 91042](https://maps.google.com/?q=9100+Tujunga+Canyon+Blvd,+Tujunga,+CA+91042) - [(818) 835-1121](tel:818-835-1121)
- Americas Children — [10924 Oro Vista Ave, Sunland, CA 91040](https://maps.google.com/?q=10924+Oro+Vista+Ave,+Sunland,+CA+91040) - [(818) 813-1984](tel:818-813-1984)
- For seniors:
  - Sunland Senior Center (senior nutrition site) — [8640 Fenwick St, Sunland, CA 91040](https://maps.google.com/?q=8640+Fenwick+St,+Sunland,+CA+91040)
  - Burns Manor Senior Dining Center — [8155 Foothill Blvd, Sunland, CA 91040](https://maps.google.com/?q=8155+Foothill+Blvd,+Sunland,+CA+91040)
